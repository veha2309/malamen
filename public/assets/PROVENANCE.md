# Prototype image provenance

All four images in this folder were generated specifically for the Malamen speculative redesign on 5 September 2026 using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. They are not photographs of the real venue and should be replaced with owner-approved Malamen photography before production launch.

- `hero.webp`: cinematic New Delhi restaurant interior at blue hour
- `day.webp`: sunlit long-lunch dining table
- `food.webp`: contemporary Indian editorial food image
- `night.webp`: cocktail and bar atmosphere after dark

The project ships only the compressed WebP derivatives. The built-in generator retains the high-resolution source masters outside the deployable application.
